# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous project.
Examples of expected behavior include constructive feedback, clear evidence,
respect for different levels of experience, and careful handling of security
reports and non-public astronomy data.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
publishing another person's private information, and knowingly submitting
code or data without the right to share it are unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, project events,
and public spaces where someone represents the mmxisf project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening,
or harmful. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented
privately, and avoid exposing reporters or affected people.

To report a conduct incident privately, use **Security > Report a
vulnerability** in this repository and begin the title with `[Conduct]`. This
channel is also identified in `SECURITY.md`. Do not include more personal or
sensitive information than is needed for the maintainers to respond.

Maintainers will make a good-faith effort to acknowledge a report within seven
calendar days. This target is not a service-level agreement.

## Attribution

This project-specific policy is informed by common open-source community
standards while keeping the enforcement contact inside the repository's
documented private reporting channel.
